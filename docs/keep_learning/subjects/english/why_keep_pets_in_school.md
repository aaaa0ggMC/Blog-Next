# Why Keep Pets in School

There is simply no need and no valid reason to keep pets in school.

Some may say, "Hey! Pets warm my heart. They give me emotional support to reduce stress." It's true that the company can ease anxiety. But just like those old ladies dancing at night with unbearable noise, sheltering pets personally in school would also ruin other students' health and focus just in exchange for your own happiness, which greatly violates moral qualities in public environment.

First and foremost, pets alway produce unpleasant odor. It's ok if you are used to it. But it may be a nightmare for specific visitors.

Secondly, the noise they make will distract others. Usually, pets are not quiet; they actively interact with surroundings and produce random noises. Noises are constant; noises are distracting; noise are so annoying!

Last but not least, were you to adopt pets just to seek companionship, what would happen when you graduate? Are they still able to play with you? It's not a question of adoption; it's a question of abandonment. No matter how big or small the possibility is, there are pets roaming in the school like ghosts.

So, keeping pets is not appropriate. To gain happiness, you can do other things obviously, as the saying goes:

All Roads Lead to Rome,

But cruel adoptions only leave pets to Roam.
